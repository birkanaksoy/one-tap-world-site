One Tap World — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-15 · iOS app

Summary

One Tap World collects no personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking. The game works fully offline.

What is stored

The app stores the following only on your device, never transmitted to any server:

  • Highest unlocked level
  • Best taps and best time per level
  • Total taps across all levels
  • Your settings (sound on/off, haptics on/off)

You can erase this data at any time from inside the app via Settings → Reset Progress, or by deleting the app.

What is NOT collected

  • Name, email, phone number, address
  • Location
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
  • Crash logs or analytics events
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera

Third-party services

None. The app makes no network requests and embeds no third-party SDKs.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
