One Tap World — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-15 · iOS app

Summary

ONE TAP WORLD is a free puzzle game supported by advertising. The app itself does not collect any personal data directly. The only data flow is via Google AdMob, which serves ads inside the app.

You will be asked once, on first launch, whether you allow tracking for personalized advertising. You can decline — the game stays free and fully playable; only the ads become non-personalized.

Stored only on your device

The app stores the following locally, never sent to any server we control:

  • Highest unlocked level
  • Best taps and best time per level
  • Total taps across all levels
  • Your settings (sound on/off, haptics on/off)

You can erase this data at any time from inside the app via Settings → Reset Progress, or by deleting the app.

Collected by Google AdMob

When you see an ad, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA, only if you granted ATT permission)
  • Crash and performance data
  • Approximate location (IP-based, regional only)
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)

Google uses this to serve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. See their policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you decline ATT on iOS 14.5+, only aggregated, non-personalized data is used.

NOT collected

  • Name, email, phone number, address
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera
  • Account info (there is no account system)

When ads appear

  • A short full-screen ad ("interstitial") appears after every third death (level failure).
  • No ads on the title screen, level select, or during active play.

Children

The app is rated 4+. AdMob is configured to comply with Google's family policies. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking: tap "Ask App Not To Track" on first launch
  • Reset progress: in-app Settings → Reset Progress
  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: revoke at any time

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — privacy policy

No other third-party SDKs are embedded.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
